The brand background texture: flat low-poly triangles running from deep rust to bright gold, ending in a corner of brand maroon.

- `lowpoly-gold.svg` is the master (vector, 76 triangles, 500×500 viewBox): scales to any size with no blur. Cleaned from `NXDFractal.svg` in the Business/Brand Drive folder; the shapes and colours are unchanged.
- `lowpoly-gold.jpg` (2000×2000) is a raster copy for tools that can't take SVG.
- Sampled range: deep `#943103`, `#a24603`, `#b26003`, mid `#c88103`, `#d69602`, bright `#efbf01`, with a `#7f0000` maroon corner.
- Use it as a full-bleed background for social graphics, stream scenes, thumbnails and print backs. It tiles poorly; crop or scale it instead of repeating it.
- Never set text directly on it: its tones swing from dark to light, so no single text colour holds 4.5:1 across it. Put text on a solid `maroon` or `gold` panel over the pattern (gold on maroon, 8:1; maroon on gold, 8:1).
- Don't recolour it, add gradients over it, or mix it with other textures.
