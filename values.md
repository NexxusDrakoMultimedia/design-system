# Values and statements

These two statements are part of the brand, not optional extras. Use the wording below; don't soften it, shorten it past the short forms, or swap in generic boilerplate.

## Acknowledgement of Country

Nexxus Drako Multimedia is made in Meanjin, on Jagera (Yagara) and Turrbal land. Every acknowledgement names both peoples, states that sovereignty was never ceded, and calls the land what it is: stolen.

**Long form** (website footer and About page, YouTube and Twitch About sections, book copyright pages, album liner notes, press kits):

> Nexxus Drako Multimedia is created on the stolen lands of the Jagera and Turrbal peoples, in what is now called Brisbane. Sovereignty was never ceded. This always was, and always will be, Aboriginal land. I pay my respects to Jagera and Turrbal Elders past and present, and to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. Colonisation didn't end; it's ongoing, and I back First Nations-led fights for land back, treaty and truth-telling.

**Short form** (email signatures, social bios, stream end cards, merch tags, video descriptions):

> Made on stolen Jagera and Turrbal land. Sovereignty never ceded. Always was, always will be Aboriginal land.

Rules:

- Name the Jagera and Turrbal peoples every time. Never write "the traditional owners of the land" without naming them.
- Write "what is now called Brisbane" (or "Meanjin") — not "Brisbane" on its own — in the long form.
- Say "stolen land" and "sovereignty never ceded". Don't replace them with "the land on which we work".
- This is an Acknowledgement, never a Welcome to Country. Only Traditional Owners give a Welcome.
- When work is made or recorded on other Country, name that Country instead or as well: Wurundjeri Woi-wurrung land for Healesville and the Yarra Valley, Wiradjuri land for Albury.
- Set it in `body` or `body-sm` on a solid ground, never over the low-poly pattern. It doesn't go in the display face.

## Queer by design

Nexxus is transfem, nonbinary, and proudly queer in most directions: a member of the LGBTQIA+ community, running a queer creative studio.

**Statement** (website About page, Discord rules header, press kit, community guidelines):

> Nexxus Drako Multimedia is queer-run and queer-made. Trans, nonbinary, intersex, asexual, aromantic, bi, pan, lesbian, gay and every other queer voice is welcome here, and actively backed — not tolerated, backed. Transphobia, intersexphobia, homophobia, biphobia, acephobia and any other bigotry get you removed, no warnings and no debate. Trans rights are human rights.

Rules:

- Write the community as **LGBTQIA+**, in full. Don't drop the I or the A.
- Use a person's own name and pronouns, always. Nexxus is she/they.
- Don't run "both sides" content on anyone's right to exist, and don't platform people who do.
- Moderators enforce the statement in every NDM space: Discord, Twitch chat, YouTube comments, GonkyRacing and tournament lobbies.

### The flag

The pride flag for every Nexxus Drako Multimedia use is the **Intersex-Inclusive Progress Pride flag**, designed by Valentino Vecchietti in 2021. It builds on Daniel Quasar's Progress Pride flag, adding the intersex flag's yellow field and purple circle (Morgan Carpenter, 2013).

- Use it in place of the six-stripe rainbow flag, the Progress Pride flag or any other version.
- Use the official artwork, never a redraw. Keep its colours, proportions and order exactly; never tint it maroon or gold, crop the chevron or put the logo over it.
- Give it clear space and its own ground, like a logo. It can sit beside an NDM lockup, not inside it.
- Alt text: "Intersex-Inclusive Progress Pride flag".
- Credit Valentino Vecchietti by name when the flag features on merch, posters, album or book art, or a Pride campaign.
- Check the flag's published terms before printing it on anything sold.
